# No Live privacy policy

AI DRAFT (Claude), 15 Oct 2026. Not yet reviewed by Jonathan.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

No Live is a browser extension and a userscript made by Hillmade. This is what it does with your data, in plain English.

**It collects nothing.** No Live does not collect, record or keep any information about you: not what you watch, search for or click.

**It stores nothing.** It saves no history, no cookies and no settings, and it does not use browser storage.

**It sends nothing.** It makes no network requests of its own. It sends nothing to Hillmade, to anyone else or to any server. It has no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

**Updates.** If you install the userscript, your userscript manager (Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey) checks GitHub for newer versions. That check is made by the manager, not by No Live.

**What it does on your device.** While you browse YouTube, No Live reads the video data that YouTube's own pages load in your browser. It uses that data to tell whether a video is a live stream, and to block it. All of this happens inside your browser. None of it leaves your device.

**Where it runs.** Only on youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com, including the embedded players on those sites. It does nothing on any other website.

**YouTube is unchanged.** YouTube's own privacy policy still covers what YouTube collects and does. No Live does not change that.

**Open source.** The code is under the MIT licence, so anyone can read exactly what it does: https://github.com/Hexakin/no-live

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page, with a new date at the top.
